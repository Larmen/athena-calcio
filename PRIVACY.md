# Athena Calcio – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Athena Calcio shows Italian football standings, results and fixtures. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and the developer receives no data from it.

## What the app sends
- **Serie A:** requests to football-data.org carry **your own API key**, which you enter yourself. The key is added only to requests to football-data.org.
- **Serie B:** requests to ESPN's public site (site.api.espn.com) carry no key or identifier.
- Team emblems are fetched from crests.football-data.org and a.espncdn.com.
Like any internet request, these servers can see your IP address and the request itself. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

## What is stored on your device
Your API key (in Android's encrypted storage), your favourite teams, your settings and a saved copy of the last data received so the app works offline. "Remove API key" in Settings deletes the key and the saved data. Uninstalling removes everything.

## Sharing
Nothing is shared with the developer or any third party other than the requests described above. The app reads theme, typeface and text size from Athena Launcher if it is installed on the same phone.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects nothing about anyone.

## Open source
